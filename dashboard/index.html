How the number is made

An implied value is a claim about one thing: what this protocol's token would be worth if it launched with its record exactly as the snapshot holds it. It is built only from the snapshot's own figures. Tokenised protocols set the price of TVL — the median market cap per TVL dollar in the protocol's category, shrunk toward the global median when the category is thin. That multiple is then moved by the protocol's own seven-day TVL growth and by its endurance: how long it has run, at size, with no token. Each live ratio is winsorised at the market's 90th percentile first, so one wild peer cannot price a whole category. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade, bridges and exchanges are priced at the global multiple on at most $5B of their TVL with a 0.05 scope factor, the reason printed on the row — they are set aside by the same rule that builds the watchlist. Every row of the board carries its own arithmetic; open any protocol to audit it.
