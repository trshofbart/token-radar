How the estimate is made (the full method)

An estimated launch value answers one question: what would this protocol's token be worth if it launched with its record exactly as the snapshot holds it? It is built only from the snapshot's own figures. Protocols that already trade set the price of locked money — the median market cap per dollar locked in the protocol's category, shrunk toward the global median when the category is thin. Each live price is winsorised at the market's 90th percentile first, so one wild peer cannot price a whole category. That price is then moved by the protocol's own seven-day growth and by its endurance: how long it has run, at size, with no token. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade, bridges and exchanges are priced at the global rate on at most $5B of their locked money with a 0.05 scope factor, the reason printed on the row — they are set aside by the same rule that builds the watchlist. Every row carries its own arithmetic; open any protocol to audit it.
